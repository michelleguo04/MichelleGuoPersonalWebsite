About

A short intro.

I'm pursuing an accelerated M.S. in Information Systems Management and a B.S. in Information Systems at Carnegie Mellon University, with a minor in Artificial Intelligence and Architecture.

My work spans machine learning, causal inference, data engineering, and AI product development — most recently building data infrastructure and ESG analytics for an intelligence agent, and researching wildfire risk and public safety power shutoff (PSPS) effects at CMU.

Outside of technical work, I explore design and visual storytelling through architecture and photography. I'm a Canadian citizen currently studying in the U.S. on F-1 status and open to relocation.
